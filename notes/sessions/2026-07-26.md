## Coherence review

- Designer asked for an honest coherence assessment of CORE-01–32. Verdict: no actual
  contradictions; five tensions flagged (no-pity RNG × friction budget; Steam Deck ×
  controller-droppable; procedural variation × targetability; pet passive benefits ×

  (CORE-20 production constraints still open while 30+ design decisions are locked).

## Refinement batch (designer responses to the five tensions — approved)

1. RNG: pity rejected permanently; dry streaks mitigated via reward breadth
   (cosmetic/collection boss drops → collection menu, not inventory). Designer notes
   variance is intentionally fun in both directions.
2. Controller: worst-case framing accepted — a relaxed multi-hour grinding input;
   never holds back real gameplay. Steam Deck reframed as contingent.
3. Procedural variation: authored bounds — fixed locations with small authored boss
   pools (3–4); roaming rares patrol authored zone+route restrictions. Assistant added
   sole-source rule (share portals across pool or keep pools variety-only); accepted.
4. Pets: designer agreed cosmetic-only if included at all.
5. Raids: designer proposed Kara/Naxx-style wings + skip items + maybe checkpoints and
   asked for honest opinion. Agreed outcome: wings [P]; player-controlled persistence
   with no time-based lockouts [P]; deterministic skip baseline (mastery shortcuts or
   trophy tokens) [T] with rare skips only as luxury; within-wing checkpoints [T].

All five recorded in GDD, Decision Register, and Living Questionnaire in one commit.

## CORE-33 — answered [L/P]

- Designer: dodging purely through movement, like RotMG — no universal dodge/defense
  button. Consolidated with binding consequences (honest dodgeability by movement alone
  for the least mobile class, movement speed as premier stat, heightened readability
  burden, death design must account for no escape action) and a class-ability boundary
  ([P]: class trees may add mobility/defense tools, never required by encounters,
  details to CORE-34). Recorded in questionnaire, GDD §8, and register; all status
  markers advanced to CORE-34 active.
